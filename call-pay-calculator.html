How the calculator works out your pay

The calculator uses the same pay rules as the Locum Tally iPhone app, in the same order. For each shift:

  1. Worked time is end minus start, minus the unpaid break. A shift that ends earlier than it starts ends the next day, so 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM is 12 hours.
  2. Rounding is applied once to the worked time, at your billing increment (actual minutes, 6, 15 or 30), rounding up or to the nearest. With round to nearest, a tie goes up and a short shift never rounds to zero.
  3. Premium hours. Billed time past the premium threshold is paid at the premium rate. The threshold is per shift. At exactly the threshold there are no premium hours.
  4. Guaranteed hours. If billed time is under the guarantee, the shift is topped up to it at the shift’s base rate.
  5. Weekend and holiday rates replace the hourly rate for the base hours and the top-up, based on the day the shift starts. A holiday beats a weekend. Premium hours stay at the premium rate.

For each call block, the stipend is paid once, by the day the call starts (holiday, then Saturday or Sunday, then Mon–Fri). Hourly call pays the call window minus the time you were called in. Called-in time is paid at the called-in rate, or your hourly rate if that’s blank. Rounding applies to the call hours and the called-in hours separately. Guarantees, premium hours and weekend rates don’t apply to call. If you take call several days in a row, add one call row per day so each day gets its stipend.

Every line is minutes × rate ÷ 60, rounded to the nearest cent, and the total is the sum of the lines. That way the total always matches the lines on your invoice.

Worked example: the sample week

Load the sample week to see these rules in action. The rate is $175 an hour, $250 after 10 hours, an 8-hour guarantee, 15-minute increments rounded up, $200 on weekends, $262.50 on holidays, and a $500 call stipend ($600 weekends, $750 holidays):

  • Mon: 7:00–5:30 with a 30-minute break is exactly 10 hours, so no premium: $1,750.00.
  • Tue: 7:00–7:10 PM with a 15-minute break is 11h 55m, rounded up to 12 hours: 10 hours at $175 plus 2 premium hours at $250 = $2,250.00.
  • Tue night call with 2h 15m called in: $500 stipend plus 2.25 hours at $175 = $893.75.
  • Thanksgiving: a 6-hour shift is topped up to the 8-hour guarantee at the holiday rate: $2,100.00.
  • Sat: 8 hours at the weekend rate: $1,600.00.

Week total: $8,593.75.

Check your contract for these terms

Call and premium terms vary widely between facilities and agencies. Before you rely on a number, check: whether the premium threshold is per shift or per week (this calculator, like the app, uses per shift); whether the guarantee applies to every scheduled shift or only when you’re sent home early; how called-in time is rounded; and which holidays count. When you know the rules, enter them once in the app and every shift is billed the same way.

Frequently asked questions

How is call pay usually calculated?

Most contracts pay a flat stipend for each call period, an hourly rate for the time you’re on call, or both. Time you’re called in to work is usually paid at your regular rate or a separate called-in rate. Enter your contract’s method under Call pay.

What happens when a shift is exactly at the premium threshold?

No premium hours. With a 10-hour threshold, exactly 10 hours is all regular time. Premium starts with the first billed minute past 10 hours.

How do guaranteed hours work?

If your billed time is below the guarantee, the shift is topped up to the guarantee at the base rate for that day. A 6-hour shift with an 8-hour guarantee pays 8 hours.

Does it handle overnight shifts and call?

Yes. If the end time is before the start time, the shift or call ends the next day. Call can run up to 72 hours, using the Call ends option.

Is my information saved or sent anywhere?

Nothing leaves your browser. If you turn on saving, your rules and entries are kept in this browser’s local storage only.

Does Locum Tally use the same rules?
