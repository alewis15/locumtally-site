Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Locum Tally iOS app (“the app”), published by Aaron Lewis of Fort Wayne, Indiana (“I”, “me”), handles information. Short version: I do not collect your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third‑party servers of mine.

Information you enter

Your business details, facilities/contracts, rates, shifts and time punches, receipts, invoices, payments and notes are stored on your device. I never receive them. Do not enter patient information; the app is not designed for protected health information.

iCloud sync

If you turn on sync, your records are stored in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit private database) under your Apple ID. That data is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. I cannot see or access it. You can turn sync off in the app and manage iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Camera and photos

If you choose to, the app uses your camera or photo library only to attach receipt images to your records. Images stay on your device and in your private iCloud if sync is on.

Notifications

Optional billing reminders (e.g. an upcoming billing day or a payment follow‑up) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to me or any server; there are no push notifications from a server.

Exports, email and sharing

When you email an invoice, share a PDF, export a CSV/IIF file for Excel or QuickBooks, or save a JSON backup, the app hands the file to the iOS mail composer, share sheet or file picker. The file goes only where you send it, and the recipient or service you choose (for example your email provider, Files, iCloud Drive or QuickBooks) handles it under its own policies. The app does not connect to QuickBooks or any accounting service directly.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. I do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple’s StoreKit on your device.

Data I collect

None. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. If you email me for support, I will receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to reply.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, or delete the app to remove on‑device data. To remove synced data, delete it in the app with sync on, or remove the app’s data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

The app is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date.
