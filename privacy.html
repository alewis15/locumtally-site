Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Locum Tally ("the app") is made by Aaron Lewis, an independent developer in Fort Wayne, Indiana, USA. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

I don't collect any data. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party code. Your records stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud database. I can't see them.

What the app stores and where

  • On your iPhone: your shifts, facilities, pay rates, invoices, payments, expenses, receipts and settings. This data is shared with the app's widgets and Live Activity on the same iPhone.
  • In your private iCloud (only if iCloud sync is on): a copy of those records, so your iPhones on the same Apple Account stay up to date. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database. Only your Apple Account can read it. Apple runs iCloud under Apple's Privacy Policy. To keep your devices in sync, iCloud may send the app silent background signals that contain no personal content.
  • Files you export or back up: PDFs, CSV files and backups go only where you send or save them, using the iPhone share sheet or the Files app.

What I don't do

  • No sign-in or Locum Tally account. I don't ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No servers of my own. The app doesn't send your data to me or to anyone else.
  • No location tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Receipt text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition. Photos and files you attach stay with your records. The app can't see the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Billing reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren't sent from a server. You can turn them off in the iPhone Settings app.

Purchases

Apple processes all Locum Tally Pro purchases. I never receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your Apple Account. The App Store may give me anonymous, combined sales reports, which don't identify you.

Email to me

If you email me for support, I use your message only to reply to you. I don't add you to a mailing list.

Patient information

Locum Tally isn't designed for patient or health information. Please don't enter patient names or other protected health information.

Children

The app is a business tool for adults. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

You can turn iCloud sync on or off in the app. You can delete records in the app, delete the app to remove its data from your iPhone, and delete the iCloud copy in the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. See Support for step-by-step instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
