Features

Built around facilities, shifts and the way locums actually get paid

General hour trackers don't know about call stipends or premium hours. Generic invoice apps don't know when you clocked in. Locum Tally does both.

Pay rules that match your contract

Set them once per facility. Every shift is priced the same way your contract says.

  • Hourly rate, plus a premium rate after a set number of hours in a shift
  • Weekend and holiday day rates
  • Call pay: a stipend (by day if needed) or hourly, plus called-in time
  • Guaranteed hours per shift and unpaid breaks
  • Billing increments: actual minutes, or 6, 15 or 30 minutes, up or nearest
  • Payment terms per facility, such as Net 30

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does Locum Tally cost?

Locum Tally is free to download. The free plan includes the time clock, widgets, Live Activities, Siri, iCloud sync, the PDF income report, 1 active facility and 1 invoice per calendar month. Locum Tally Pro is $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, with a 2-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Your price is shown in the App Store before you buy.

Who is Locum Tally for?

It's built for 1099 locum providers who bill facilities directly: CRNAs and other anesthesia providers, locum physicians, nurse practitioners, PAs and other independent clinicians. Any independent contractor who bills clients by the shift or by the hour can use it. A "facility" is simply the client you bill.

Can it handle call pay, premium hours and billing increments?

Yes. Each facility has its own hourly rate, an optional premium rate after a set number of hours in a shift, weekend and holiday rates, call pay (a stipend, by day if you like, or hourly, plus called-in time), guaranteed hours per shift, and time rounding: actual minutes or 6, 15 or 30 minutes, rounded up or to the nearest. Unpaid breaks come off before rounding.

Can I email invoices from the app?

Yes. Pick a facility, choose its unbilled shifts and create a PDF invoice with your business details, the facility's Bill To contact and PO number. Email it to the billing contact from the app, or save, print or share the PDF. You can add expenses and receipt photos to an invoice.

Does Locum Tally work with QuickBooks?

With Pro you can export invoices as a QuickBooks Online CSV or a QuickBooks Desktop (IIF) file, plus an Excel-ready CSV and an accountant package. Find them in Settings → Accounting. There is no live QuickBooks sync.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

No account is needed. Your records stay on your iPhone and, if you turn it on, sync through your own private iCloud. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer can't see your data. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Can I clock in from the Lock Screen?

Yes. While you're clocked in, a Live Activity shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with a Clock Out button. There are Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Siri phrases such as "Clock in with Locum Tally."

Is there an Android or web version?

Not yet. Locum Tally is an iPhone app and needs iOS 17 or later. If you'd use an Android version, join the Android waitlist and we'll let you know if it launches.

Does Locum Tally give tax advice?

No. It's a record-keeping tool. Check your taxes, invoices and contracts with a qualified professional.
