What is a 1099 invoice?

A “1099 invoice” is simply the invoice an independent contractor sends a client. It isn’t an IRS form. The 1099 part refers to Form 1099-NEC, which the client may file after the year ends to report what it paid you. Your invoice comes first. It’s the business record that asks to be paid, and it’s how you prove your gross receipts later. The IRS lists invoices among the records that support your income (IRS: What kind of records should I keep).

What’s in this template

  • From and Bill To blocks for your name or business name (as on your W-9) and the client’s legal name and billing contact.
  • Invoice number, invoice date, due date and payment terms, plus PO or contract number and service period.
  • A services table with date, description, hours or quantity, rate and amount. In Excel and Google Sheets, each amount is a formula (hours × rate, rounded to the cent).
  • An expenses section for reimbursable costs, with its own subtotal.
  • Total due and payment instructions: ACH details, check address or a payment link.

How to use it in Excel, Google Sheets, Word or PDF

Excel: open the .xlsx and type over the sample text. Enter hours and rates in the yellow cells. Amounts, subtotals and the total due fill in on their own. Save a copy for each invoice, such as INV-0012.xlsx.

Google Sheets: download the .xlsx, open Google Sheets, choose File → Import → Upload and pick the file. Choose “Create new spreadsheet”. The formulas carry over. Then use File → Download → PDF to send it.

Word or Google Docs: the .docx has the same layout as an editable table. Word doesn’t total the rows for you, so check your math, or let the invoice generator do it.

PDF: print it and fill it in by hand, or use it as a layout guide.

Tips for contractors

  • Number invoices in order and never reuse a number. It makes follow-up and taxes easier.
  • Write a real due date. “Due Dec 9, 2026” gets paid sooner than “Net 30”.
  • Keep your SSN off the invoice. Give your taxpayer ID on a W-9, not on every invoice.
  • Track what’s unpaid and follow up a few days after the due date.
  • Set aside tax. Nobody withholds from 1099 pay. See how self-employment tax changes your take-home with the 1099 rate calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official IRS 1099 invoice form?

No. The IRS doesn’t publish an invoice form for contractors. Any clear invoice with your details, the client’s details, the work, the amounts, the total and the due date will do. Form 1099-NEC is a separate tax form the client may file after the year ends.

Does the template work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Import the .xlsx into Google Sheets with File → Import → Upload. The line, subtotal and total formulas keep working.

Should I put my Social Security number on a 1099 invoice?

No. Give your taxpayer ID to the client on Form W-9. Many contractors get a free EIN from the IRS so they don’t have to share their SSN at all.

Is the template really free?

Yes. No sign-up or email is needed. Download, edit and reuse it as often as you like.

What’s the difference between a 1099 and an invoice?

You send an invoice to get paid for your work. A 1099-NEC is a year-end tax form the client may file to report what it paid you. Keep your invoices so you can check any 1099-NEC you receive.
