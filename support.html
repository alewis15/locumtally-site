What Locum Tally does

Locum Tally is an iPhone app for independent (1099) locum providers. It lets you:

  • clock in and out of shifts, or add them by hand;
  • set pay rates for each facility (hourly, daily, overtime, call and callbacks);
  • build PDF invoices with expenses and receipts;
  • record payments and track what you're still owed;
  • run income reports and export them for your accountant.

iCloud sync

How does sync work?

Sync uses your own private iCloud database. It keeps your iPhones that are signed in to the same Apple Account up to date. Turn it on or off in Settings → iCloud sync. Sync is free.

I can't see your synced data, and neither can anyone else.

My other iPhone isn't updating.
  • Check that both phones are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on (Settings → your name → iCloud).
  • Open Locum Tally on both phones, then tap Sync now in Settings → iCloud sync.
  • If iCloud storage is full, sync pauses until you free up space.
  • If the app says some changes conflict or are waiting, open it on the other phone so the related records can arrive. Nothing is deleted while it waits.
Do I need iCloud to use the app?

No. Without iCloud, everything stays on this iPhone. Save an external backup now and then (see below).

Subscriptions and restoring purchases

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: the time clock, widgets and Live Activity, 1 active facility, 1 invoice a month, the PDF income report, iCloud sync and backups.

Locum Tally Pro adds unlimited facilities and invoices, QuickBooks and CSV exports, and receipt scanning with the camera. It costs $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, with a 2-week free trial.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In the app, open the Pro screen and tap Manage subscription. Or go to the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Locum Tally.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds come from Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Offer codes

How do I redeem an offer code?

In the app, tap Redeem a code on the Pro screen or in Settings, then enter your code. Apple shows the offer and handles the purchase.

You can also redeem a code in the App Store app: tap your profile picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

Deleting your data

How do I delete records?

Swipe left on a shift, expense or invoice to delete it. Invoices you've already sent can only be deleted if they were made by mistake. This keeps your billing history accurate. You can archive a facility you no longer work with.

How do I delete everything?
  • On your iPhone: delete the Locum Tally app. This removes its data from that iPhone, including the widgets.
  • In iCloud: go to the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Storage) → Locum Tally, then delete its data. Turning sync off alone doesn't remove the iCloud copy.
  • Backups and exports you saved are files you control. Delete them in the Files app or wherever you saved them.

There's no Locum Tally account and no server copy, so you don't need to ask me to delete anything.

Exports and backups

What can I export?
  • Free: PDF invoices and invoice packets with receipts, and the PDF income report.
  • Pro: an accountant package, an Excel-ready CSV, a payment reconciliation CSV, a QuickBooks Online CSV and a QuickBooks Desktop (IIF) file.

Exports open the iPhone share sheet, so you can save to Files, AirDrop, email or print. Live QuickBooks sync isn't available yet.

How do I back up and restore?

Go to Settings → Backup & recovery → Save external backup. This saves everything, including receipts, to Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose Import external backup. The app also keeps your last five saves on this iPhone as automatic recovery copies.

Other questions

Is Locum Tally tax or accounting advice?

No. It's a record-keeping tool. Check your taxes, invoices and contracts with a qualified professional.

Can I enter patient information?

No. Please don't. Locum Tally is for your own business records, not patient records.
