Common questions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?
iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Locum Tally.
I bought Pro but it isn’t showing.
Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.
What’s free?
1 facility and 1 invoice per month. Pro ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr, 2‑week free trial) unlocks unlimited facilities and invoices plus extra tools.
How do I sync to my iPad or new phone?
Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on, then turn on sync in the app’s Settings on each device.
How do I back up?
Settings → Backup & recovery → export a JSON backup to Files or iCloud Drive.
Refunds?
Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
